R² near 0 means pH is essentially independent of the other eight parameters in this dataset - that is an honest result, and exactly why the app falls back to median imputation unless the model clearly beats the mean baseline.

Why there is no accuracy here

The dataset has no "anomaly" labels, so accuracy/recall cannot be computed honestly for anomaly detection. Instead we validate the Isolation Forest by (a) inspecting the score distribution, (b) checking that injected faults in TEST FIXTURES are caught (see the pytest suite), and (c) combining the model score with simple physical rule checks whose reasons a human can read.
