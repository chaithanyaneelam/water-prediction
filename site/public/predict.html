Radar values are normalized as value ÷ guideline limit (pH as distance from 7 in the 6.5-8.5 band). 1.0 = at the limit.

Guideline limits shown are typical WHO / BIS / EPA reference values and are for guidance and charts only. Many samples in the training dataset exceed these limits yet are labelled potable, so limits never create labels or model features. Predictions are statistical estimates from the Kaggle Water Potability dataset and are not a substitute for laboratory analysis.
